Questions and answers

What is QR Code Halo?

An iPhone QR code and barcode scanner and QR creator by Lumi Studio. It scans 20 barcode formats from the camera or a photo and acts at once: links open and Wi-Fi codes bring up the Join prompt. It also creates nine kinds of QR codes: text, link, Wi-Fi, contact, email, phone, SMS, location and calendar event.

Who is it for?

Anyone who scans codes often and wants them to open straight away, keep a searchable history, read codes from screenshots and photos, or make their own Wi-Fi and contact QR codes. History is unlimited, with favorites, search, filters, sorting and CSV export.

How is it paid for?

Scanning with the camera or the photo picker is unlimited for everyone, with no scan count. All nine QR creators, unlimited local history and full batch-audit reading are included. One optional Lifetime Pro purchase adds saving and acting on batch results, batch creation and batch CSV, committed backup and restore, high-resolution PNG, PDF and SVG export, and custom Aurora styles. No subscription.

How is it different from other scanners?

It works with photos and several codes in one frame, supports batch scanning with duplicate suppression, and keeps every scan in an on-device history. If you want to look a code over, an optional Security Check in History breaks the link into parts on the device and labels it Safe, Caution or Blocked; javascript:, data: and file: codes never open. Scans open instantly by default, and you can turn off “Run action after scanning” to check codes first.

What does it not do?

It is not a document scanner: it does not scan pages, perform OCR or keep a PDF document library. PDF export contains QR artwork only. The Security Check uses no network reputation lookup.

Scan, and it opens.
