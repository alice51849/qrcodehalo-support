Scan a QR code and it opens instantly, then find it later in History

Point, scan, done. QR Code Halo acts the moment it reads a code: links open and Wi-Fi codes bring up the Join prompt. There is nothing extra to tap. Every scan is also kept in History on your device, so when you want that menu, parking page or Wi-Fi code again, it is a search away.

Scan and it opens

  • Links open at once.
  • Wi-Fi codes bring up the Join prompt.
  • 20 barcode formats, including QR, Micro QR, Aztec, Data Matrix, PDF417, EAN-13, UPC-A and Code 128.
  • Camera or photo: scan live, or recognize codes in a photo, several codes in one frame.
  • Zoom and flashlight for small or dim codes.

Look back in History

Every scan outside batch mode is saved on your device unless Skip saving to history is on. To find a code again:

  1. Open History.
  2. Search, filter or sort by type, date, format, tag or category.
  3. Tap the code to see it again.

Worried a code you scanned might be quishing (QR code phishing)? Open it in History and tap Security Check, which reviews it on your device.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when I scan a code with QR Code Halo?

It acts at once: links open and Wi-Fi codes bring up the Join prompt. It reads 20 barcode formats from the camera or a photo, including several codes in one frame.

Where do my scans go?

Every scan outside batch mode is saved on your device unless Skip saving to history is on.

How do I find a code I scanned last week?

Open History. It has no item cap and offers favorites, search, filters and sorting by type, date, format, tag or category.

Can I export my scan history?

Yes. History can be exported as CSV.

Is scanning limited?

No. Scanning with the camera or from photos is unlimited for everyone, with no scan count.

Can I make my own QR codes?

Yes. You can create nine kinds: text, link, Wi-Fi, contact, email, phone, SMS, location and calendar event.
